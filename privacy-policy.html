Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 20, 2026

PRISMLAB ("we", "us", "our") sells handmade acrylic enclosures online and ships them across the Philippines. This policy explains what personal information we collect when you use our website, why we collect it, who we share it with, and what choices you have. We follow the Philippine Data Privacy Act of 2012 (Republic Act No. 10173).

1. Who we are

PRISMLAB is a small online shop based in the Philippines. For any privacy question or request, email us at your-email@example.com.

2. What we collect

  • Account details if you sign up as a member: full name, email address, and password (stored in encrypted form by our login provider. We cannot see your password).
  • Order details: name, mobile number, email address, delivery address (street, barangay, city, province, ZIP code), the items you ordered, and your order total.
  • Map location: the pin you drop on the map when checking out, if you choose to drop one. The address text you type may be sent to a map search service to find it.
  • Voucher use: which vouchers you received and used.
  • Payment status: whether your QR Ph payment succeeded. We do not see or store your bank or e-wallet login, card number, or PIN.
  • Basic device data that your browser sends to our hosting and service providers, such as IP address and browser type.

You can also order as a guest without creating an account. We still need your order and delivery details to fulfil the order.

3. Why we use your information

  • To process and deliver your order, and to calculate delivery fees.
  • To confirm your payment.
  • To send you transactional emails about your order: order confirmation, payment received, packed, shipped (with tracking number), and delivered. Also account emails like email confirmation and password reset.
  • To manage your member account, order history, and vouchers.
  • To answer your questions and handle returns or complaints.
  • To keep our website secure and prevent fraud or misuse.

We do not sell your personal information. We do not send marketing emails unless you clearly agree to receive them, and every such email will include a way to unsubscribe.

5. How long we keep it

We keep order records for as long as needed to handle your order, returns, and complaints, and to meet legal and accounting requirements. Member accounts stay until you ask us to delete them. After that, we delete or anonymize your information unless the law requires us to keep it.

6. How we protect it

Passwords are handled by our login provider and are never stored in plain text. Our database limits each member to seeing only their own orders and vouchers. Secret keys are kept on our server side and never placed on this public website. No online service is completely risk-free, but we take reasonable steps to protect your data.

7. Your rights

Under the Data Privacy Act, you have the right to:

  • be informed about how your data is used;
  • access a copy of your personal information;
  • correct information that is wrong or out of date;
  • object to or withdraw your consent for certain uses;
  • ask us to delete or block your information, where allowed by law;
  • file a complaint with the National Privacy Commission (privacy.gov.ph).

To use any of these rights, email us at your-email@example.com. We may ask you to confirm who you are before we act on a request.

8. Children

Our shop is meant for adults. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 18 without a parent or guardian.

9. Cookies and local storage

We use your browser's local storage to remember your shopping cart and to keep you logged in. We do not use advertising trackers.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The effective date at the top will show when it was last changed. Continuing to use our website after a change means you accept the updated policy.
